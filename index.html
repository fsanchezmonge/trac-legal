Privacy Policy

trac.

trac records how your training sessions felt. This policy explains exactly what it stores, where, and how to get it back or delete it.

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Who is responsible

trac is an independent project run by an individual developer, who is the data controller for the information described here. Contact: fernandosanchezmp@gmail.com.

What trac does not store

  • No name, email address, or password. Accounts are anonymous — the app creates a random identifier and nothing more.
  • No Strava access tokens on any server. They are held in your device's Keychain and never transmitted to trac's database.
  • No activity content. No routes, GPS traces, maps, pace, power, heart rate, or Relative Effort. From Strava the app reads only each session's name, sport, duration and start time; everything else Strava sends is ignored on your device.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party trackers. None. There is no advertising identifier, no attribution SDK, and no usage telemetry.

Health data, and your explicit consent

How hard training felt and how your body responded is health-related data — a special category under Article 9 of the GDPR. trac processes it only on the basis of your explicit consent, given when you connect Strava and start recording ratings. You can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting your data in the app, which erases everything (see below). Withdrawing does not affect processing that already happened.

Where it is stored

Ratings are stored in a Postgres database hosted by Supabase in the European Union (Frankfurt). Every row is protected by row-level security, so the database will only return records belonging to your own account.

Who else processes it

  • Supabase — hosting and database, EU region, under their Data Processing Addendum.
  • Apple — delivers push notifications. Apple receives your device token, the notification text — "How did that feel? Rate your session while it's fresh." — and the Strava ID of the session, so that tapping the notification opens the right one. It contains no training data, not even the sport or duration.

Strava is not a processor for trac. You authorise trac to read your activity list from Strava, and that data travels from Strava to your device. Your use of Strava is governed by Strava's own privacy policy.

Your data is not sold, rented, shared for advertising, or used to train machine-learning models.

No automated decisions about you

trac shows short descriptive observations after you submit a rating — for example, that an easy session felt off, or that three of your last five sessions did — and, on its home screen, arrows showing how your rated sessions have moved compared with the period before. These are simple comparisons against your own history. trac does not diagnose, does not score your readiness, does not tell you what to train, and makes no automated decision that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

How long it is kept

Until you delete it. Ratings are a long-term record and are meant to accumulate — a year-old entry is the point, not clutter. There is no automatic expiry. Deleting removes everything immediately.

Your rights

  • Access and portability — Settings → Export my data produces your complete record as JSON, and your training log as CSV, on demand, without asking anyone.
  • Erasure — Settings → Delete everything removes all ratings, skips, the patterns worked out from them, your profile, and your account. It cascades in the database and clears the Strava tokens from your device. It cannot be undone.
  • Rectification, or deleting a single entry — the app deliberately does not let you edit a rating after submitting it, because revising a rating against what you later saw would corrupt the measurement. If you need a specific entry corrected or removed, email fernandosanchezmp@gmail.com and it will be done manually.
  • Objection and withdrawal of consent — stop using the app and delete your data; both are available at any time without contacting anyone.
  • Complaint — you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children

trac is not directed at children under 16 and should not be used by them.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Strava credentials are stored in the device Keychain, encrypted by iOS and never sent to trac's servers. Database access is restricted per-account by row-level security. No system is perfectly secure, but no credential and no activity content is held server-side, which limits what a breach could expose.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will change and, where the change affects how your data is processed, you will be asked to consent again.
